About this guide

A starting point, not a complete census.

Britain is home to around two thousand masajid and prayer rooms. This site features a selection of well-known Sunni and Shia centres so you can compare facilities at a glance. Each listing is labelled by tradition. Ratings are illustrative community scores for demonstration. Always confirm prayer times and access with the mosque itself.

Facilities listed include women’s prayer space, wudu, parking, wheelchair access, madrasah, library, funeral services, nikah, and cafés where publicly known.
